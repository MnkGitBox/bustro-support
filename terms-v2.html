§ 1

About Bustro

Bustro is a free, ad-free public-transit utility for Singapore that provides:

  • Bus arrival information sourced from the Singapore Land Transport Authority ("LTA") DataMall;
  • Bus stop and bus service search, route visualisation, and a metro map;
  • A favourites and Home/Lock Screen widget for quick access to commute information;
  • Alight Reminder: an opt-in feature that follows your bus ride and alerts you before your alighting stop, with a live countdown shown as a Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity;
  • Track Arrival: an opt-in Live Activity showing a live countdown and alerts for a bus arriving at a stop;
  • My Journeys: saved trips that you can re-activate, synced through your iCloud account;
  • Weather information sourced from the Singapore Government's data.gov.sg APIs;
  • Traffic-incident alerts and an opt-in push-notification subscription system;
  • A Commute Feed of transit-related updates, including (a) user-submitted messages, (b) official advisories from the Bustro team, and (c) automated LTA service alerts;
  • An on-device, AI-generated transit-mood headline produced using Apple's Foundation Models.

Bustro does not require an account, does not charge a subscription, and contains no in-app purchases or advertising.

§ 5

Location data

Bustro is more useful when you grant location permission, but is functional without it. Location is processed on your device to find nearby stops, calculate walking distances, display the closest favourite stop on the widget, and, if you set an Alight Reminder, follow your ride so you can be alerted before your stop. Alight Reminder offers the optional Always location permission so it can keep working in the background; background location runs only while a reminder is active and stops when the ride ends. We do not transmit your location to our servers or to LTA, the Singapore Government, BusRouter SG, or any other third party. See the Privacy Policy for the full description.

§ 6

Push notifications

If you grant notification permission, we may send you traffic-incident alerts for the categories you subscribe to, notifications about new Commute Feed updates, and the alerts belonging to features you start yourself: Alight Reminder ("prepare to alight" / "time to alight") and Track Arrival ("arriving soon" / "arriving now"). Live Activities you start are updated by push while they run. You can unsubscribe at any time in Settings → Notifications → Bustro, by changing your in-app subscription preferences, or by ending the reminder or tracker. We deliver pushes via Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
